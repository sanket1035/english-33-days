- Studied today: Daily routine vocabulary. Grammar: present simple, frequency words (usually, always).
- Practised today: Spoke my daily routine from wake-up to sleep. Practised the IELTS answer for "What is your daily routine like?"

  - "walk two minutes" -> "It takes two minutes to walk"
  - "wind down by go out" -> "wind down by going out"

  - Said two wake-up times. Use one real time.
