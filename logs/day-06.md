# Day 06: Questions

- Studied today: Question words (what, where, when, why, how). Grammar: question word order, polite indirect questions, modals should / could / would.
- Practised today: Recorded a 1-minute video answering 5 questions about myself. Practised polite and formal English. Made my own sentences with should, could and would.
- New words (5): courteous, tactful, articulate, ambiguous, inquisitive
- Mistakes:
  - "When I go to the restaurant" -> "When I go to a restaurant"
  - "I am an articulate person, in my native language" -> "I am already quite articulate in my native language"
  - "So that's why" -> "That's why"
  - "now finally I think that I learn something new" -> "Now I finally feel that I have actually learned something new"
  - "watching movie" -> "watching a movie"
  - "Would you like a tea?" -> "Would you like some tea?"
  - "No, I could drink kesar milk" -> "No, I'll drink kesar milk" / "I'd rather have kesar milk"
  - "I feel like stomach ache" -> "I have a stomach ache"
- Plan for tomorrow: Day 7, hobbies and free time. Grammar: like / love / enjoy + verb-ing.
