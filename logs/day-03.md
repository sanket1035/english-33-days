# Day 03: Home and area

- Studied today: Home and area vocabulary. Grammar: there is / there are.
- Practised today: Spoke about my village house in Savkheda and my rented flat in Nashik. Used there is / there are. Practised the IELTS answer about house or flat.
- New words (5): accommodation, spacious, convenient, amenities, locality
- Mistakes:
  - "too near" -> "very close"
  - "My neighbor is my uncle and aunt" -> "My neighbours are my uncle and aunt"
  - "the second floor, it has also three rooms" -> "three rooms on the upper floor"
  - "locate the center" -> "in the center of the village"
  - "a really convenient" -> "really convenient"
  - "it is a big space" -> "it is spacious"
- Plan for tomorrow: Day 4, daily routine. Grammar: present simple. Words: wake up, breakfast, usually, always, sleep.
